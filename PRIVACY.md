# Privacy

Countdown (`io.github.tqnl.countdown`) reads events from Android Calendar Provider after you grant calendar access. It uses event titles, dates, times, and calendar names to let you select events and display countdowns. Selected event identifiers, occurrence times, widget names, and display preferences are stored locally on the device.

Countdown does not operate a server, include advertising or analytics, or transmit your calendar to its developer. The release does not request Internet access. Your calendar app and account provider may sync events independently under their own privacy policies.

Tapping a widget opens the event in an app you choose or your default calendar handler, passing its event reference and times. Open calendar similarly requests a date view in a calendar app. These actions are initiated by you.

You can revoke calendar access in Android settings, remove widgets, or clear/uninstall Countdown to remove its local settings. Removing a Countdown widget does not delete its calendar event. App backup is disabled.

For questions, use https://github.com/TQNL/Countdown/issues and avoid posting private calendar details.
